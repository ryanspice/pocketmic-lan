The native apps and the marketing website have different boundaries. This page explains the supplied product’s local-audio design and the behaviour of this website build.

Website policy draft for this build · 9 September 2026

The PocketMic app connection

PocketMic is designed to send microphone audio from Android to a Windows receiver on a trusted private network. No PocketMic account or cloud relay is required for that local connection. The supplied app policy states that the apps do not send telemetry.

Pairing and connection settings are stored locally by the native apps. The supplied policy describes encrypted pairing-key storage on Android and a local receiver settings file on Windows. Review diagnostic exports before sharing; never publish the pairing key or QR code.

The local transport boundary ends at Windows. Discord, Teams, OBS, Zoom, or another destination app may send, process, stream, or record the audio according to its own settings and policies. “No cloud relay” does not make an online meeting private to your LAN.

This website build

The delivered site uses self-hosted CSS, JavaScript, and image files. It does not include Google Analytics, advertising tags, external badge requests, third-party fonts, embedded donation widgets, microphone access, or tracking pixels. It does not ask for your pairing key or device IP address.

The setup checklist saves your checked steps in sessionStorage only after you interact. The data stays in that tab’s session and can be cleared using “Reset this checklist.” No account, identifier, audio recording, or device details are stored by that feature.

Campaign parameters may be carried between internal page links, after restricting their format and length. The JavaScript emits local browser events describing link clicks and self-reported checklist actions. No analytics service receives those events from this build.

The hosting provider may process ordinary server-request logs. Server/CDN logging, injected host scripts, retention periods, and any future analytics integration must be reviewed by the site operator before this draft is published as the final policy.

External destinations

GitHub hosts release downloads and source code. VB-Audio hosts its separate audio driver. Official app-support pages and the optional Buy Me a Coffee link are external sites with their own terms. No third-party widget is loaded merely by opening this website.

Software licence and support boundary

The supplied project identifies PocketMic software as MIT licensed. The software is provided under its licence terms without a warranty. Consult the licence in the pinned source; this marketing page is not a separate warranty or service-level commitment.

Use PocketMic only on a trusted private LAN. Do not port-forward the receiver. Encryption is a transport property, not an independent security certification. Test the software on your own devices before relying on it.

VB-CABLE is a separate third-party product with its own donationware licensing and installation requirements. PocketMic is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord, OBS, Microsoft, Zoom, or VB-Audio. Those names identify possible audio destinations or setup dependencies.
