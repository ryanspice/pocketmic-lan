PocketMicSetup guide

Your first sound check

Get from installed
to heard.

Follow the audio path one step at a time. This page is a checklist, not a device detector — it does not listen to your microphone or inspect your apps.

Android: install the debug-signed APK. Windows: extract the self-contained ZIP and run PocketMicReceiver.exe. Use the same release for both. Get the two downloads.

Connect Android and Windows to the same trusted private network. A wired PC and Wi-Fi phone are fine when the network permits local device-to-device traffic. Avoid isolated guest networks.

For Discord, OBS, or another app’s mic input, install VB-CABLE from VB-Audio (opens in a new tab) and follow its restart instructions. It is a separate third-party driver. You can skip it only for a direct receiver-to-headphones test.

Open the receiver and check the connection details. Use QR pairing where available, or enter the PC address, audio port, and the same pairing key on Android. Keep the key and QR code private. Allow the project’s documented firewall rules on Private networks only.

Start the receiver and the phone’s microphone. Speak and look for input-level activity and increasing receiver packet counts. Neither is proof that your final calling or recording app has the correct input.

In PocketMic Receiver choose CABLE Input. In your destination app choose CABLE Output as its microphone. Keep your listening output on your usual headphones. Run the app-specific test below.

The receiver plays into VB-CABLE through CABLE Input. Your app reads from it through CABLE Output. Those two names are not interchangeable.

Discord: input device + mic test

Open Voice & Video, choose CABLE Output as the input device, then use Mic Test to listen. Leave the output device on your headphones. Official Discord guide (opens in a new tab).

OBS: scene input + test recording

Add an Audio Input Capture source and select CABLE Output. Avoid capturing the same device globally and in the scene. Record and review a short sample for level and sync. Official OBS guide (opens in a new tab).

Teams / calling apps: microphone + test call

Choose CABLE Output as the calling app’s microphone. In Teams, use Settings → Devices and the audio settings, then make a test call where available. For other apps, use their own input selector and test. Official Teams guide (opens in a new tab).

Other Windows apps: select the recording endpoint

Choose CABLE Output in the app’s microphone selector where supported. If the endpoint has been renamed, match it to VB-CABLE’s recording side. Test the actual app before relying on it.

Avoid feedback while testing.

Keep the receiver’s speaker-monitoring option off when you do not need it. Use headphones for a direct listening test, then check the actual destination app.

Now listen to the result.

Once your destination app can hear you, continue to the final sound-check notes. Only you can confirm whether the native-app setup worked.
