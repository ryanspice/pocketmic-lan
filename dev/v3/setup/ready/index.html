The final check

Make sure it’s
your voice they hear.

Reached this page from the setup checklist? You reported that your app can hear the phone. Opened it directly? Run the checks below first. This website has not verified your connection.

Speak, pause, listen.

Check ordinary speech and a moment of silence in a short test call or recording. Notice level, noise, and any audible delay.

Check the real session.

Test the app you actually plan to use. For video, review audio/video alignment. Keep a known-working mic available.

Finish the session deliberately.

Stop streaming in PocketMic when you are finished. Change the destination app’s input back when you switch to another microphone.

Keep connection details private.

Do not post your pairing key, QR code, or screenshots containing connection details. Review diagnostic exports before sharing.

Help improve the next setup.

Useful feedback includes your phone model, Android version, Windows version, PocketMic version, destination app, and a description of the symptom. Do not include secrets, a pairing QR code, or a full private-network address.
